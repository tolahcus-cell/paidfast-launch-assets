# PaidFast Brand & Content Guide (for agents)

Read this before you design, write or schedule any PaidFast marketing content.
Machine-readable colours: `palette.json` · Swatches: `palette-swatches.png` · Logo: `paidfast-logo-icon.png` · Font: `Manrope-Variable.ttf`
Public raw base: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tolahcus-cell/paidfast-launch-assets/main/brand/`

## 1. What PaidFast is
Invoicing SaaS for **freelancers, creators and small businesses in Kenya** (use this exact phrase in intros).
Quotes → invoices → receipts → statements, payment links, automatic reminders, recurring invoices. Site: **paidfast.online**.

## 2. Colours
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep green (background) | `#04302A` | Base of every visual. Never use white or light backgrounds for social posts. |
| Teal glow | `#14CFB5` | Primary accent, "Fast" in the wordmark, start of the headline gradient |
| Emerald | `#08957F` | Secondary glow, success/paid states |
| Gold | `#F5C949` | CTA pills, end of the headline gradient, small highlights. **Use sparingly.** |
| Mint | `#D6ECE6` | Body and secondary text on green |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Headlines, "Paid" in the wordmark |
| Ink | `#0E1814` | Text on gold or light surfaces |
| Card / Card line | `#0A423A` / `#22685C` | Cards and borders on green |
| Charcoal | `#161819` | Phone frames only |

Video renders use slightly brighter variants (teal `#18D6BA`, emerald `#109670`, mint `#BEE8DE`). They do the same job.

**Headline keyword gradient:** teal `#14CFB5` → gold `#F5C949`, left to right. Use it on one or two words per headline, never on a whole paragraph.

**Aurora background:** start from the deep green `#04302A` and add large blurred radial glows:
- teal at the top-left, about 37% opacity
- emerald at the bottom-right, about 59% opacity
- an optional small gold glow at the top-right, about 12% opacity

Mirror the positions from slide to slide for variety.

## 3. Logo & wordmark
- Show the logo **big**: at least 10% of the frame width on feed posts, and at least 14% on Story or Reel openers and end cards.
- Wordmark: "**Paid**" in white plus "**Fast**" in teal, Manrope Bold, with no space between.
- Every carousel, video end card and Story shows both the logo and paidfast.online.

## 4. Typography
Use Manrope everywhere:
- Headlines: Bold or ExtraBold
- Subheads: SemiBold
- Body: Medium

Use sentence case and short lines.

## 6. Content rules (all set by the founder)
1. Add **#CreativeEconomy #Jitume** to every post on every channel.
2. **No founder face** and no founder-story content.
3. **Never call reminders a Starter-only feature.** New sign-ups get to try them.
4. It is fine to show Starter features, such as recurring invoices.
5. **Don't write "secure payment by Pesapal".** More payment methods are coming, so keep payment wording generic ("pay by card or M-Pesa via the payment link").
6. Payment facts:
   - Pesapal is the only live gateway (card and M-Pesa through Pesapal; KES/UGX/TZS/USD).
   - Direct M-Pesa checkout is **not live**.
   - PayPal is **not connected**.
   - Foreign clients pay **by card through Pesapal**.
7. Vary the formats: before/after splits, time-lapses, maps, and AI-generated human stories. Avoid repeating "phone on green background".
8. For AI-generated people: keep one reference portrait so hair and clothing stay consistent; never show duplicate hands or limbs; set `isAiGenerated` on X and Instagram; add "(Characters in this video are AI-generated.)" on LinkedIn.
9. Claims must be accurate. Check them against the product before posting.

## 7. Workflow
1. Build the content, then **send it to the founder for review. Do not schedule anything in Buffer until he approves it.**
2. Host assets in `tolahcus-cell/paidfast-launch-assets`, using commit-pinned raw URLs, under `weekN/<day>-<topic>/`.
3. Schedule through Buffer (timezone Africa/Nairobi):
   - Channels: LinkedIn `6ac40bd76a5c39ccb624e24f`, X `6ac411866a5c39ccb62541c1`, Instagram `6ac409d06a5c39ccb624d36d`.
   - The free plan allows at most 10 scheduled posts at once.
